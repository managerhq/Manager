Chart of Accounts Builder

This extension builds a profit & loss chart of accounts inside a business. Pick a business above, switch to the Chart of accounts tab, choose a structure, review the preview, and create the groups and accounts with a single click.

Structures

Simple — two top-level groups, Income and Expenses, with a flat list of common accounts under each. Best for sole traders and small businesses.

Detailed — separate groups for Income, Cost of sales, Operating expenses, Other income and Finance costs, with Gross profit and Operating profit subtotals and expense subgroups, for a full profit & loss statement. (The final Net profit (loss) total is built into every statement.)

The builder only creates what's missing — groups, subtotals and accounts that already exist (matched by name) are left untouched, so it is safe to run more than once.
